Hereabouts shows the plants that grow wild in your county and where to buy them.

Enter your ZIP code to see the trees, shrubs, wildflowers, grasses, and ferns native to your county. It covers the lower 48 states. Filter by sun, soil moisture, and height to find the ones that suit your spot.

Each plant has photos and care notes. A chart shows the months it is in leaf and in bloom. "Where to buy" lists the local nurseries and online sellers that carry it.

Save plants to your Garden. Every Saturday morning the app lists the jobs your saved plants need that week. The timing follows your county's frost dates. Tick each job off when it's done.

Every plant and photo works without signal.

You don't need an account, and there are no ads or subscription. Some seller links are affiliate links. If you buy through one we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
